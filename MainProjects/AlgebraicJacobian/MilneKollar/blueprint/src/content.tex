The route follows the rigidity and Albanese arguments of Milne and Mumford
\cite{milne-av,mumford-av}, the divisor
conventions of Hartshorne \cite{hartshorne-ag}, and the
Picard and Hilbert--Quot constructions of Kleiman and Nitsure
\cite{kleiman-picard,nitsure-hilbert-quot}.  The collected source
volume is \cite{fga-explained-2005}.  Its adelic
Riemann--Roch input is inspired by Papaioannou \cite{papaioannou-rr}; the
sheaf, cohomology, and derived-category foundations follow the corresponding
chapters of the Stacks Project \cite{stacks-project,stacks-algebra,stacks-cohomology,
stacks-cohomology-schemes,stacks-derived-categories,stacks-modules,stacks-modules-sites,
stacks-schemes,stacks-sheaves,stacks-sites,stacks-varieties}.  The scheme-theoretic
background also uses EGA and the algebraic-group references recorded below
\cite{ega-i,ega-iv2,conrad-2002,borel-1969,matsumura-1987,
atiyah-macdonald-1969,weibel-1994}.
