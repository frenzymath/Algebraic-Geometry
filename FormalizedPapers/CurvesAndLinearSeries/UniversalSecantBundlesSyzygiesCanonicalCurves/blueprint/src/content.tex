\href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/2003.05849}{direct PDF}.
\textbf{Summary.} The paper develops relative secant bundles for canonical curves, reproves Voisin's theorem,
and identifies the final syzygies in even genus, resolving the geometric syzygy conjecture there.
\textbf{Reading warning.} This blueprint rephrases and distills the original paper; read it alongside the original.
