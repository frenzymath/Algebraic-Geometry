\chapter*{About This Blueprint}

This project follows Robin Hartshorne, \emph{Algebraic Geometry}, Graduate
Texts in Mathematics 52 (Springer-Verlag, 1977). The publisher's eBook/PDF
edition is available
\href{https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/978-1-4757-3849-0.pdf}{from Springer}.

Hartshorne develops algebraic geometry from varieties to schemes, sheaves,
cohomology, and duality, with major applications to curves and surfaces. The
appendices treat intersection theory, transcendental methods over the complex
numbers, and the Weil conjectures.

This blueprint is a source-faithful, independently worded mathematical
distillation, not a replacement for the book. It retains definitions,
hypotheses, constructions, numbered results, dependencies, and proof arguments
while omitting examples, exercises, motivation, and other pedagogical prose.
\textbf{It should be read alongside the original source PDF}, which supplies
the full explanations, examples, and surrounding context. The \emph{dcref}
and \emph{source} metadata identify each item's printed location, and proofs
are placed in separate environments after their statements.

The blueprint covers all five chapters and the three mathematical appendices:
varieties, schemes, cohomology, curves, surfaces, intersection theory,
transcendental methods, and the Weil conjectures.
