\chapter*{About This Blueprint}

This project follows Part 7, \emph{Algebraic Stacks}, of the Stacks Project.
The complete current Stacks Project PDF is available
\href{https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/download/book.pdf}{here}.

This part develops algebraic stacks, examples and sheaves on stacks,
representability criteria and Artin's axioms, Quot and Hilbert spaces,
properties and morphisms of stacks, limits, cohomology, derived categories,
and the geometry of algebraic stacks.

This blueprint is a source-faithful hgraph adaptation for dependency tracking,
not a replacement for the Stacks Project. It retains the mathematical
statements, proofs, and official chapter order while adapting presentation and
recording dependencies. \textbf{It should be read alongside the original
source PDF.} Permanent Stacks tags in the blueprint provide direct coordinates
for comparison with the continuously maintained online source.
