Algebraic Geometry is a source-faithful formalization workspace.

The repository connects carefully transcribed mathematical sources with dependency tracking and a future Lean formalization layer. The graph is organized for comparison, reuse, and gradual formal verification.

Source library

The Stacks Project is imported through Parts 1-8, covering 109 chapter blueprints. The reference shelf also includes Mumford and Milne on abelian varieties and Hartshorne on algebraic geometry, curves, and cohomology. Eight additional source projects are scaffolded with copy-ready Luna/Codex prompts for the next transcription pass.

Formalization direction

Shared infrastructure is kept separate from source material. The Algebraic Jacobian is the flagship direction, with Milne-Kollar and Picard-Albanese blueprint routes under MainProjects/AlgebraicJacobian; the abelian-variety and curve references provide the surrounding theory.

Current snapshot

The manifest registers 21 routes: 13 active projects and eight planned source projects. The workspace graph currently indexes more than 17,000 source statements; Lean declarations are added incrementally and are never claimed before they exist.
