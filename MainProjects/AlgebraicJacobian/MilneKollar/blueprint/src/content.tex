\chapter*{About This Blueprint}

This blueprint was prepared by an AI from the references listed in
\texttt{refs.bib}, principally Milne, Mumford, Kleiman, Nitsure, Hartshorne,
and the Stacks Project.  It is a compact mathematical roadmap for the
Milne--Koll\'ar construction of the Jacobian: formalization-critical
intermediate definitions and lemmas are retained, while implementation
diaries and routine bookkeeping are omitted.  It does not claim that the
displayed results are formalized.  Printed source numbers are recorded by
\texttt{\textbackslash dcref}; citations use the BibTeX keys in \texttt{refs.bib}
and internal dependencies use \texttt{\textbackslash ref}.
