\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
This blueprint follows Allen B. Altman and Steven L. Kleiman, \emph{Compactifying
the Picard Scheme}, \emph{Advances in Mathematics} 35 (1980), 50--112
(MR0555258).  The article record is available through
\href{https://doi.org/10.1016/0001-8708(80)90043-2}{its DOI}.

It retains the definitions, hypotheses, constructions, numbered and unnumbered
assertions, and proof arguments in Sections 1--8.  Examples, exercises,
bibliography, index material, and historical or pedagogical prose are omitted.
The \emph{dcref} value records the printed source number and \emph{source}
records the PDF page.
