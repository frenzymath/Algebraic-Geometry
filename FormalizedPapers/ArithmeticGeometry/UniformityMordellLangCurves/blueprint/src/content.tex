\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{uniformity-mordell-lang-curves-about}

\textbf{Edition and PDF.} ArXiv preprint \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.10276}{arXiv:2001.10276};
\href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/2001.10276}{direct PDF}.
\textbf{Summary.} The paper proves uniform bounds for rational points on curves in terms of genus, field degree,
and Jacobian rank, together with uniform bounds for geometric torsion in Abel--Jacobi images.
\textbf{Reading warning.} This blueprint rephrases and distills the original paper; read it alongside the original.
