\chapter*{About This Blueprint}

This blueprint follows B. Fantechi, L. Göttsche, L. Illusie, S. L. Kleiman,
N. Nitsure and A. Vistoli, \emph{Fundamental Algebraic Geometry: Grothendieck's
FGA Explained} (AMS Mathematical Surveys and Monographs 123, 2005).  The
publisher's book page is available at
\href{https://bookstore.ams.org/SURV/123}{the American Mathematical Society}.

It retains the mathematical definitions, hypotheses, constructions, numbered
and unnumbered assertions, displayed calculations, and proof arguments in the
book's nine chapters.  Examples, exercises, answers, bibliography, index
material, and historical or pedagogical prose are omitted.  The \emph{dcref}
value records the printed number in the source and the \emph{source} value
records the corresponding PDF page.
