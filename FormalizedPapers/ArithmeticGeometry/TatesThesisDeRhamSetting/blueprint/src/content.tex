\href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/2107.11325}{direct PDF}.
\textbf{Summary.} The paper computes $D$-modules on the loop space of the affine line and identifies them with
ind-coherent sheaves on rank-one de Rham local systems equipped with a flat section.
