\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
This blueprint follows Hideyuki Matsumura, \emph{Commutative Ring Theory},
Cambridge Studies in Advanced Mathematics 8 (Cambridge University Press,
1986; revised English edition).  The publisher's book page is available at
\href{https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9781139171762}{Cambridge University Press}.

It retains the definitions, conventions, unnumbered assertions, numbered
results, and proof arguments in mathematical source order.  Examples,
exercises, solutions, bibliography, index material, and historical or
pedagogical prose are omitted.  The \emph{dcref} value records the printed
source number and \emph{source} records the PDF page.
