\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{refined-brill-noether-theory-hurwitz-spaces-about}

\textbf{Edition and PDF.} Hannah K. Larson, \emph{A refined Brill--Noether
theory over Hurwitz spaces} (2020),
\href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.08597}{direct PDF}.

\textbf{Summary.} The paper refines Brill--Noether loci on a general
$k$-gonal curve by stratifying line bundles according to the splitting type
of their pushforward to $\pp^1$. It proves expected dimension bounds,
smoothness, and non-emptiness of the resulting strata using degenerations to
chains of elliptic curves and universal splitting-locus formulas.

\textbf{Reading warning.}
This blueprint is a rephrased and distilled mathematical guide, not a
verbatim transcription. Read it alongside the original paper for complete
statements, proofs, conventions, and references.
