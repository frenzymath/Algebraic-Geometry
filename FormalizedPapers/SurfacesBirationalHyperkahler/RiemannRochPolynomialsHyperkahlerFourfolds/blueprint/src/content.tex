\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{riemann-roch-polynomials-hyperkahler-fourfolds-about}


\textbf{Edition and PDF.} ArXiv preprint \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.08152}{arXiv:2201.08152}; \href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.08152}{direct PDF}.

\textbf{Summary.} It shows that a hyper-Kahler fourfold with two suitable integral degree-two classes has the topology, Fujiki constant, and Riemann--Roch polynomial of $K3^{[2]}$, and hence that deformation type.

\textbf{Reading warning.} The chapters distill and rephrase the source for formalization. Read them alongside the original paper; the blueprint is not a substitute for the source.
