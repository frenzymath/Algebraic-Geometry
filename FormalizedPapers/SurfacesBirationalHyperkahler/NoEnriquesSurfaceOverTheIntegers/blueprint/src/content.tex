\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{no-enriques-surface-over-the-integers-about}


\textbf{Edition and PDF.} ArXiv preprint \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.07025}{arXiv:2004.07025}; \href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.07025}{direct PDF}.

\textbf{Summary.} It proves that no family of Enriques surfaces exists over the ring of integers, combining the local system of numerical divisor classes with arithmetic surface theory, elliptic fibrations, and point-counting arguments.

\textbf{Reading warning.} The chapters distill and rephrase the source for formalization. Read them alongside the original paper; the blueprint is not a substitute for the source.
