\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{hitchin-fibrations-abelian-surfaces-pw-about}


\textbf{Edition and PDF.} ArXiv preprint \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.11885}{arXiv:1909.11885}; \href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.11885}{direct PDF}.

\textbf{Summary.} It studies the topology of Hitchin fibrations through degenerations of Hilbert--Chow morphisms on abelian surfaces, proving the genus-two case of $P=W$ and locating tautological classes in the predicted perverse pieces.

\textbf{Reading warning.} The chapters distill and rephrase the source for formalization. Read them alongside the original paper; the blueprint is not a substitute for the source.
