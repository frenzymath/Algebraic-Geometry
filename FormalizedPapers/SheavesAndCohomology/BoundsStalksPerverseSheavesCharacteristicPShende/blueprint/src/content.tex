\label{bounds-stalks-perverse-sheaves-characteristic-p-shende-about}


\textbf{Edition and PDF.} ArXiv preprint \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.04850}{arXiv:1907.04850}; \href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.04850}{direct PDF}.

\textbf{Summary.} It proves a characteristic-$p$ bound for stalks of perverse sheaves from characteristic-cycle intersections and applies it to theta loci in hyperelliptic Jacobians and a function-field mixing conjecture.

\textbf{Reading warning.} The chapters distill and rephrase the source for formalization. Read them alongside the original paper; the blueprint is not a substitute for the source.
