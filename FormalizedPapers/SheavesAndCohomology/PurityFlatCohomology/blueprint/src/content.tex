\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{purity-flat-cohomology-about}


\textbf{Edition and PDF.} ArXiv preprint \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.10932}{arXiv:1912.10932}; \href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/1912.10932}{direct PDF}.

\textbf{Summary.} It proves Gabber--Thomason purity for flat cohomology of finite flat group schemes over complete-intersection local rings, via perfectoid descent and prismatic Dieudonne theory, with consequences for Brauer and etale cohomology.

\textbf{Reading warning.} The chapters distill and rephrase the source for formalization. Read them alongside the original paper; the blueprint is not a substitute for the source.
