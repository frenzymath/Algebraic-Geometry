\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
This blueprint follows Tom Leinster, \emph{Basic Category Theory}, Cambridge
Studies in Advanced Mathematics 143 (Cambridge University Press, 2014).  The
open-access PDF is available at
\href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/1612.09375}{arXiv:1612.09375}.

It retains the definitions, universal properties, numbered and unnumbered
mathematical assertions, diagrams expressed algebraically, and proof arguments
in source order.  Examples, exercises, bibliography, index material, and
non-mathematical exposition are omitted.  The \emph{dcref} value records the
printed source coordinate and \emph{source} records the PDF page.
