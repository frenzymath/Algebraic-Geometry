\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{motivic-invariants-birational-maps-about}


\textbf{Edition and PDF.} ArXiv preprint \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.07389}{arXiv:2207.07389}; \href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/2207.07389}{direct PDF}.

\textbf{Summary.} It constructs groupoid-valued invariants of birational maps in the Kontsevich--Tschinkel and truncated Grothendieck groups, then applies them to questions about the Grothendieck ring, $L$-equivalence, and Cremona groups.

\textbf{Reading warning.} The chapters distill and rephrase the source for formalization. Read them alongside the original paper; the blueprint is not a substitute for the source.
