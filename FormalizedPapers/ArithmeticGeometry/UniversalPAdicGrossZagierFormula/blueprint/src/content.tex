\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{universal-p-adic-gross-zagier-formula-about}

\textbf{Edition and PDF.} ArXiv preprint \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.00045}{arXiv:2001.00045};
\href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/2001.00045}{direct PDF}.
\textbf{Summary.} The paper constructs a universal Heegner class over a Hida family, proves a universal $p$-adic
height formula, and derives Gross--Zagier, Bloch--Kato, and Iwasawa-theoretic consequences.
\textbf{Reading warning.} This blueprint rephrases and distills the original paper; read it alongside the original.
