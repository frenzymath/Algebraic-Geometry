\chapter*{About This Blueprint}

This blueprint was prepared by an AI from the references listed in
\texttt{refs.bib}, principally Milne, Mumford, Kleiman, Nitsure, Hartshorne,
and the Stacks Project.  It is a compact mathematical roadmap for the
Milne--Koll\'ar construction of the Jacobian; it is not a transcription of
every intermediate lemma and it does not claim that the displayed results
are formalized.  Printed source numbers are recorded by \texttt{\textbackslash dcref};
citations use the BibTeX keys in \texttt{refs.bib}.
