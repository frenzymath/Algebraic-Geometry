\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{intersection-complexes-unramified-l-factors-about}


\textbf{Edition and PDF.} ArXiv preprint \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.03943}{arXiv:2009.03943}; \href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/2009.03943}{direct PDF}.

\textbf{Summary.} It computes intersection complexes and nearby-cycle stalks for arc spaces of a large class of spherical varieties, describes them by Kashiwara crystals, and obtains Plancherel densities as ratios of local unramified $L$-values.

\textbf{Reading warning.} The chapters distill and rephrase the source for formalization. Read them alongside the original paper; the blueprint is not a substitute for the source.
