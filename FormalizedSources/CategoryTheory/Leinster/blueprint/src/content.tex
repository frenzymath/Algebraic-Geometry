This blueprint is a source-faithful mathematical transcription of Tom Leinster,
\emph{Basic Category Theory}, arXiv:1612.09375v2 (2025), under the project
slug \texttt{leinster-basic-category-theory}.  It follows the mathematical
chapters in order and omits only examples, exercises, bibliography, index, and
non-mathematical exposition.  Source anchors use PDF pages as
\texttt{page-NNNN}; for the body, printed page $p$ is PDF page $p+8$.
