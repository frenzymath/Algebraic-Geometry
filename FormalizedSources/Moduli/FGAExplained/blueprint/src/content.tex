\chapter*{About This Blueprint}

This is an independent, source-faithful mathematical blueprint for B. Fantechi,
L. Gottsche, L. Illusie, S. L. Kleiman and N. Nitsure, \emph{Fundamental
Algebraic Geometry: Grothendieck's FGA Explained} (AMS Mathematical Surveys and
Monographs 123, 2005).  It follows the collected volume in book order and keeps
the chapter and section boundaries and the printed numbering of all retained
mathematical statements.  Historical discussion, examples used only for
motivation, exercises, answers, bibliography and index are omitted; mathematical
assertions in those contexts are retained where they are used later.

The PDF page is ten greater than the printed book page.  Every chapter file has
page-level source anchors and the private ledger under
\texttt{.blueprint-work/fga-explained/} records the visual Luna pass for all 352
physical pages.  The text layer was used only to locate material; formulas,
quantifiers and diagrams were checked against rendered pages.
