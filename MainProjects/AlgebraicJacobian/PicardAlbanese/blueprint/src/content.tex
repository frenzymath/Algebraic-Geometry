\chapter*{About This Blueprint}

This blueprint was prepared by an AI from the references listed in
\texttt{refs.bib}, principally Kleiman, Milne, Mumford, Hartshorne, Nitsure,
and the Stacks Project.  It is a concise mathematical roadmap for the
etale-Picard and Albanese construction of the Jacobian.  It keeps the
formalization-critical intermediary definitions and lemmas, while omitting
implementation diaries, Lean declarations, and routine proof bookkeeping.
Printed source numbers are stored in \texttt{\textbackslash dcref}; bibliography
citations use the BibTeX keys in \texttt{refs.bib}; internal statements are
linked with \texttt{\textbackslash ref}.
