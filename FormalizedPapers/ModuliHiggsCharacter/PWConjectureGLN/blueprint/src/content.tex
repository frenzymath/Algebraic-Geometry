\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{pw-conjecture-gl-n-about}


\textbf{Edition and PDF.} ArXiv preprint \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.02568}{arXiv:2209.02568}; \href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/2209.02568}{direct PDF}.

\textbf{Summary.} It proves the $P=W$ conjecture for $\mathrm{GL}_n$ in every rank and genus by combining strong perversity for tautological classes, vanishing cycles, global Springer theory, and curious hard Lefschetz.

\textbf{Reading warning.} The chapters distill and rephrase the source for formalization. Read them alongside the original paper; the blueprint is not a substitute for the source.
