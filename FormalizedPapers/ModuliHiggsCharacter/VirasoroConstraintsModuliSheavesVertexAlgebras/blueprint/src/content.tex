\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{virasoro-constraints-moduli-sheaves-vertex-algebras-about}


\textbf{Edition and PDF.} ArXiv preprint \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.05266}{arXiv:2210.05266}; \href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/2210.05266}{direct PDF}.

\textbf{Summary.} It recasts sheaf-theoretic Virasoro constraints as primary states in Joyce's vertex algebra, proves their invariance under wall-crossing, and establishes the constraints for broad moduli spaces by reduction to rank one.

\textbf{Reading warning.} The chapters distill and rephrase the source for formalization. Read them alongside the original paper; the blueprint is not a substitute for the source.
