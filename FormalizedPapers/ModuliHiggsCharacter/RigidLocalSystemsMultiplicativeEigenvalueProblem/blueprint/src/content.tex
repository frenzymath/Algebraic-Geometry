\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{rigid-local-systems-multiplicative-eigenvalue-problem-about}


\textbf{Edition and PDF.} ArXiv preprint \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.12457}{arXiv:2005.12457}; \href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/2005.12457}{direct PDF}.

\textbf{Summary.} It constructs unitary irreducible rigid local systems from quantum Schubert calculus and strange duality, relates them to the multiplicative eigenvalue problem for $\mathrm{SU}(n)$ and KZ equations, and derives rank bounds.

\textbf{Reading warning.} The chapters distill and rephrase the source for formalization. Read them alongside the original paper; the blueprint is not a substitute for the source.
