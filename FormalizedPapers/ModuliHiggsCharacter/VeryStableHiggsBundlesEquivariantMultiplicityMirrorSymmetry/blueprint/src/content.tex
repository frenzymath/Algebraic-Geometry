\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{very-stable-higgs-bundles-equivariant-multiplicity-mirror-symmetry-about}


\textbf{Edition and PDF.} ArXiv preprint \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.08583}{arXiv:2101.08583}; \href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.08583}{direct PDF}.

\textbf{Summary.} It develops very stable Higgs bundles, computes multiplicities of components of the global nilpotent cone, and connects the result to mirror symmetry using equivariant indices, Hecke transforms, and Fourier--Mukai methods.

\textbf{Reading warning.} The chapters distill and rephrase the source for formalization. Read them alongside the original paper; the blueprint is not a substitute for the source.
