\chapter*{About the formalization companion}

This companion preserves the detailed intermediary definitions, constructions,
and proofs used while developing the formalization.  It is intentionally not
the indexed public route: the public text groups these ingredients into a
book-style argument.  There is no intended one-to-one correspondence between
its nodes and Lean declarations; a single mathematical statement may require
many Lean declarations and auxiliary proofs.
