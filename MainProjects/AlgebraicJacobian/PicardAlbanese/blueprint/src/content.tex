\chapter*{About This Blueprint}

This blueprint was prepared by an AI from the references listed in
\texttt{refs.bib}, principally Kleiman, Milne, Mumford, Hartshorne, Nitsure,
and the Stacks Project, together with the source-faithful blueprint and its
formalization dependency structure.  It retains the
formalization-critical intermediate definitions, constructions, and proofs;
only implementation metadata, run-history notes, and genuinely mechanical
transport lemmas are suppressed.  It is a mathematical roadmap, not a claim
that every displayed statement is already formalized.
Printed source numbers are stored in \texttt{\textbackslash dcref}; bibliography
citations use the BibTeX keys in \texttt{refs.bib}; internal statements are
linked with \texttt{\textbackslash ref}.
