\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{mumford-about}

This project follows David Mumford, \emph{Abelian Varieties}, revised second
edition (1974), with appendices by C. P. Ramanujam and Yuri Manin. A complete
scan of the source PDF is available
\href{https://wstein.org/edu/Fall2003/252/references/mumford-abvar/Mumford-Abelian_Varieties.pdf}{here}.

Mumford develops the analytic theory of complex tori and theta functions,
then reconstructs the theory algebraically for varieties and schemes. The
book treats the dual abelian variety, Poincare bundles, finite group schemes,
endomorphism algebras, polarizations, theta groups, and l-adic
representations. Its appendices prove Tate's theorem over finite fields and
the Mordell--Weil theorem.

This blueprint is a source-faithful, independently worded mathematical
distillation, not a substitute for the book. It retains the definitions,
hypotheses, statements, dependencies, and proof arguments needed for
formalization while omitting exercises, examples, and pedagogical prose.
\textbf{It should be read alongside the original source PDF}, which provides
the full exposition and context. The \emph{dcref} and \emph{source} metadata
identify the corresponding divisions and pages of Mumford's text.

The blueprint covers all four chapters and both appendices. The appendix
credits are preserved for Ramanujam's finite-field Tate theory and Manin's
Mordell--Weil theory.
