\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{canonical-representations-surface-groups-about}

\textbf{Edition and PDF.} ArXiv preprint \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.15352}{arXiv:2205.15352};
\href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/2205.15352}{direct PDF}.
\textbf{Summary.} The paper proves that low-rank complex representations of punctured surface groups with finite
mapping-class orbit have finite image, with applications to Hodge-theoretic and arithmetic conjectures.
\textbf{Reading warning.} This blueprint rephrases and distills the original paper; read it alongside the original.
