\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
This blueprint transcribes the mathematical content of Hideyuki Matsumura,
\emph{Commutative Ring Theory}, Cambridge Studies in Advanced Mathematics 8
(Cambridge University Press, 1986; revised English edition).  The complete
local PDF is the authoritative source.  Page blocks below preserve source
order and provide physical-page evidence; examples, exercises, bibliography,
and index pages are identified in the private page ledger and omitted from the
mathematical chapter ranges.  Numbered source statements receive their exact
printed coordinate in \verb|\dcref| when identified during the page audit.
