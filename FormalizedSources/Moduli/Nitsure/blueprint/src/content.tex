\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
This blueprint follows Nitin Nitsure, \emph{Construction of Hilbert and Quot
Schemes}, in \emph{Fundamental Algebraic Geometry: Grothendieck's FGA
Explained}.  The author's open-access article and PDF are available at
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0504590}{arXiv:math/0504590}.

It retains the definitions, hypotheses, constructions, numbered and unnumbered
mathematical assertions, and proof arguments of the six sections.  Examples,
exercises, bibliography, index material, and historical or pedagogical prose are
omitted.  The \emph{dcref} value records the printed number in the source and
the \emph{source} value records its PDF page.
