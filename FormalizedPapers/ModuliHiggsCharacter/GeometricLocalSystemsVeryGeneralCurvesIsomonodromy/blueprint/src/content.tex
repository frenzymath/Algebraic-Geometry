\chapter*{About This Blueprint}
\label{geometric-local-systems-general-curves-isomonodromy-about}


\textbf{Edition and PDF.} ArXiv preprint \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.00039}{arXiv:2202.00039}; \href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/2202.00039}{direct PDF}.

\textbf{Summary.} It proves a lower bound of $2\sqrt{g+1}$ for the rank of a non-isotrivial geometric local system on a suitably general pointed curve, using stability of flat bundles under isomonodromic deformation.

\textbf{Reading warning.} The chapters distill and rephrase the source for formalization. Read them alongside the original paper; the blueprint is not a substitute for the source.
